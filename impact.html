Impact

ReVision aims to help people notice fading focus early, so they can rest, adjust or refocus before it affects their work.

Who We Want to Help

ReVision is for anyone who experiences brain fog or fading focus, whatever their age, job or situation. Here are a few examples.

Students

Long hours of study, homework and exams.

People at Work

Anyone who needs steady concentration, from office work to technical jobs.

Surgeons

People whose work depends on sustained concentration, where a lapse matters.

...and many more

Anyone who has felt their thoughts go foggy, for any reason. If that sounds like you, ReVision is for you.

  • Parents
  • Teachers
  • Researchers
  • Drivers
  • Night-shift workers
  • Athletes
  • Gamers
  • Creators

How We Will Know It Works

We plan to measure whether ReVision helps with these questions:

Alert accuracy

Do the alerts match how people actually feel?

Time focused

Do people stay focused for longer?

Task speed

Do people finish tasks faster?

These are goals for testing, not results. ReVision is in the research stage and has not yet been tested with users.
