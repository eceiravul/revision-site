About Us

Lucid is a student team building ReVision, smart glasses that help people notice when their focus is slipping. We are taking part in the Conrad Challenge 2026-27.

Our Story

Our team formed in August 2026. In October we chose to work on eye-based focus tracking, after reading research on what the eyes can tell us about mental effort. Since then each of us has been studying a different part of the problem.

Our Mission

To help people notice, early and kindly, when their focus is slipping, using sensors built into everyday glasses.

Our Vision

A world where nobody has to push through mental fog without knowing it.
