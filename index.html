What is focus drift?

Focus rarely switches off. It fades. You re-read the same line, lose the thread, and notice only minutes later. "Brain fog" is an everyday word for this foggy feeling, not a medical diagnosis.

Everyday tiredness

Attention dips after long study or work, and a break usually helps.

Brain fog

A broader feeling of foggy thinking. It has many possible causes, and ReVision does not try to diagnose them.

Turning ordinary glasses into a focus coach!

Early Notice

Sensors in the frame follow your eye signals and your surroundings, so a change in attention can be noticed early.

Your Own Normal

ReVision compares you with your personal baseline, not with an average person.

Gentle Suggestions

When something looks off, a short suggestion arrives on your phone. You stay in charge.
